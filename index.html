From the founding in 2019 to the Dirty War — dates and names as recorded in the Central Committee archives.

2019THE UNION IS FOUNDED

January 9th 2019. Leon Gazi and Shadow Hokaj found the U.S.S.R. mock-government on Discord. Five years of stable rule will follow.

2023THE PREMIER SYSTEM

March 6th 2023. Leon establishes the elected Premiership — head of state over the Council of Commissars. Pakistani Gazi takes the first term; BluMan will serve the longest at 60 days.

2024ASSASSINATION

April 23rd 2024. Leon Gazi is cut down by KGB officers under Alexei K. Vasiliev. By his written will, Felix Dzerzhinsky — the Iron Felix — takes power.

2024BATTLE FOR THE KREMLIN

RGES Red Guards clash with the KGB around the Kremlin. Shadow Hokaj, Joseph Stalin, Heydar Aliyev and Casreor Ricci fall — until Nuristani reinforcements under Pakistani Gazi turn the tide at the cost of his life.

2024THE DIRTY WAR

Seven months of treachery and oath-breaking. The Felix–Robert split, the Anti-Leon Pact and recession — ending in the November Catastrophe, with Felix comatose and claimants carving up the legacy.
